# Every month, held out in turn

Generated by `scripts/report_monthly.py` with `--report` from the residuals `--fit` stored under `data/processed/monthly/`; every figure below is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier.

## What this is

The paper quotes two validation months because the design of 2 September fixed two forward splits, December and July, as the proxies for the two ranking months. A forward fit cannot validate January, the training year's first month, and a forward fit of February or March would train on one or two months and mean little. So here each month of 2025 is held out in turn and the model is trained on the other eleven: the same rows every month, a design that is **not forward in time**. Every held-out figure below was fitted on months that come after the month it scores as well as before it.

The model is the submitted rung, `+ plan differences` (`SUBMISSION_KEY`), one fit per leg at the ladder's seed (offset 0 on `BOOSTER_PARAMS["random_state"]`), under `taxiout.models.BOOSTER_PARAMS`. Inside every leg, as in the ladder: the record-quality term of `taxiout.tails` is fitted on the training months, the rung is fitted on `taxiout.models.fit_rows` (training, unflagged, and the recorded-taxi tail over 4 h, `TAIL_EXCLUSION_ADOPTED_H`, out of the fit on the fitting side only), and the shrunk encoding is recomputed from those rows. The submission itself is a seed average fitted on all twelve months; this report fits single seeds.

**This report decides nothing.** It changes no parameter, no feature and no rule, and nothing that ships is fitted from it. It is read for one thing: how the submitted rung's error is distributed over airports and months.

The constants the script holds: the twelve months (`MONTHS`), the seed offset (`SEED`), the six forward figures it checks against `reports/baseline.md` (`REF_FORWARD`), and the airport the term is expected at (`TERM_AIRPORT`, LIRF).
