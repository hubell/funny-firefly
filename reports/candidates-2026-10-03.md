**The rows every figure is over** are the validation rows that neither the record-quality term nor the plan-shift rule covers, both fitted on each split's training rows as `taxiout.tails` fits them, because the booster predicts no other row of what ships. The floors were measured over the rows that exclude the term alone, and are kept as printed. Beside every figure, in its own columns, the same reading over those rows whose recorded taxi-out is four hours or less: a diagnostic, which decides nothing.

**How a condition is read.** Every contrast is the candidate's RMSE minus the reference's over the same rows of the same split, both at the same seed, so negative is better. It is read at 2 decimals, the precision at which `reports/baseline.md` prints every contrast it reads against a floor and every floor: "better by more than the floor" asks the printed gain to exceed the printed floor, and "not worse by more than the floor" asks the printed loss not to. The interval beside each is the day-clustered percentile interval of `taxiout.validation.cluster_bootstrap`, 1,000 draws, one draw of days for both sides; it is printed and decides nothing.
