# The last three blocks at Fiumicino

Generated by `scripts/report_fiumicino.py` from the held-out-month ladder's stored residuals of the twelve held-out months; nothing is refitted, every figure is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier. **It decides nothing.** The design is `reports/monthly_ladder.md`'s: each month of 2025 held out in turn, the model trained on the other eleven, so not forward in time.

Every figure is over the rows the record-quality term does not cover, as the scorer's rule implies. *Change in squared error* is the block's sum of squared residuals minus the rung's it is read against, over the cell's rows, in millions of s²; negative means the block helps. *Share* is that change as a fraction of the block's year-level change over the group the table is for, so a group's shares add to 100% over a partition. *Change in RMSE* is the block's RMSE minus the other rung's over the same rows. Where a cell holds recorded taxi-outs over 4 h, the second reading follows in brackets: the change in RMSE over the rows of 4 h or less, a diagnostic, with the count it leaves out. A cell under 30 rows prints its count only.

The partitions: the held-out month; the flight-plan record as `taxiout.tails` reads it (`LOBT_flt` present), which is the reading of `reports/monthly_ladder.md`'s Annex D; the plan-lag bands of `reports/residual.md`, take-off minus `EOBT_1_flt` in minutes, with `no estimate` and `before the estimate` as their own rows; and the ICAO airline designator as `taxiout.callsign` reads it off `FLIGHT_mvt`, an aggregate over an operator's departures and not an identifier of any of them, as `reports/residual.md` prints it.
