Generated by `scripts/report_istanbul.py` from the five-seed average's stored residuals (`reports/monthly_seeds.md`), the training and ranking files and the METAR cache; every figure is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier. **It decides nothing.**

## What is read

The error is the five-seed average's, the model that ships, on the held-out-month splits: each month of 2025 scored by a fit on the other eleven, so not forward in time. RMSE and bias (prediction minus recorded taxi-out) are over the rows the record-quality term does not cover; at LTFM it covers 0 rows in the year, and 0 rows record more than 4 h, so the second reading over the rows of 4 h or less equals the first everywhere and is printed once.

The weather hours are `taxiout.metar.regime_by_hour` on the cached reports under `data/raw/metar/`, per UTC day: an hour is freezing, in precipitation or in low visibility (the band of under 1,500 m, `vis_band` 2) as the model's own weather block reads it, and *hours* counts the hours with a reading. The plan lag is take-off minus `EOBT_1_flt`, the mean over `taxiout.exposure.departure_rows`: departures unflagged by the record-quality term and carrying a flight-plan record, the lag windowed on its own to -30 to 120 min. The arrivals' control is `taxiout.exposure.arrival_rows` at LTFM: the mean taxi-in between 1 and 60 min, and the mean lateness, landing minus schedule within 2 h either way. The term is fitted on the whole training year and predicted on both sides, as `scripts/report_congestion.py` does for its *Regime exposure*.

Beside each February day, after the slash, the same day of the month in the other eleven months of 2025, pooled: the error over all their rows, the means over all their rows, the departures and the weather hours as a mean per day.
