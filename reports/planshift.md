# The plan-shifted departures, evaluated

Generated by `scripts/report_planshift.py` with `--report` from the training and ranking files, the record-quality term of `taxiout.tails` and the held-out-month fits' stored residuals (`reports/monthly.md`); every figure is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier. **It decides nothing.**

## The rule, as fixed on 2 October

Implemented from the pre-registration of 2 October (the working note's sha256 `d62b48f32d3ba9da07aa346cb04a2560feaff9664a9734228d7523e669dda9a1`, published as `reports/preregistered-2026-10-02.md`, which was checked at its frozen digest before anything was read): the class is a departure not covered by the record-quality term whose take-off minus `EOBT_1_flt` and minus `LOBT_flt` both exceed 4 h (`TAIL_EXCLUSION_ADOPTED_H`), both estimates present; *long* is a training departure of the class whose recorded taxi-out exceeds the same 4 h; the share is `(long + 1) / (n + 2)` over the class's departures in the rows a fit is given; the prediction is `share x lag + (1 - share) x rest_mean`, the lag being take-off minus `LOBT_flt` and the rest mean the mean recorded taxi-out of that airport's training departures with a flight-plan record and a recorded taxi-out of 4 h or less. It is substituted for the booster's prediction on the class's rows, after the term. It reads `EOBT_1_flt`, `LOBT_flt`, `MVT_TIME_UTC_mvt`, `ADEP_mvt` and, at fit time, the recorded taxi-out; never `AOBT_3_flt`. A flight-plan record is read as `taxiout.tails` reads it, `LOBT_flt` present, and the rest mean is taken over every such training row of 4 h or less, the term's rows included, as the pre-registration words it.

## The one ranking departure

One departure of the ranking set is in the class: LIRF, Jul 2026, take-off minus `LOBT_flt` 86,820 s (24.12 h), minus `EOBT_1_flt` 23.97 h, minus the schedule 26.27 h. The rule fitted on the full year predicts 0.6000 x 86,820 + (1 - 0.6000) x 1,144.2 = **52,549.7 s** (52,550 s after the submission's rounding). v6 predicts **3,120 s** for it, read from `data/submissions/funny-firefly_v6.parquet` through the template's identifier, which is not printed. The difference is +49,430 s. What the departure recorded is hidden, and nothing here says which is right.
One reading the pre-registration leaves open is whether the rest mean counts the term's own rows. Read literally it does, and that is what is implemented. Leaving them out would give a LIRF rest mean of 1,144.2 s instead of 1,144.2 s, and move the prediction by +0.0 s.

## What this evaluation can show

In the pre-registration's own words: "In the training year the class holds three departures, so this evaluation shows what the rule does to those rows and nothing more: it is a statement of the arithmetic and not independent evidence that the rule is right." Each held-out month that holds one of the three fits the rule on the other two, so the share it applies was computed from the very rows the rule was written after seeing. Nothing here settles whether the rule is right for the 2026 departure, which no data available to the entry can settle.
