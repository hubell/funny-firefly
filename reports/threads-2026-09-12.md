# The booster's thread count, and which rungs it changes

Generated by `scripts/fast_lane.py --report` from the 9 runs recorded under `data/processed/fastlane/`, which are gitignored: 114 fits in all, made on 2026-09-12 to 2026-09-13, over the december split and the july split. LightGBM 4.7.0, from the 2 of them that record it. Every figure below is computed from those records.

The ladder's stored residuals are the reference throughout. Each fit is subtracted from `data/processed/residuals/<split>_<rung>.parquet` and the largest absolute difference over the validation rows is what the tables give, in seconds of taxi-out time. At or below 1e-06 s that is the last bits of a sum taken in another order; above it the fit grew a different set of trees. The reference condition is `BOOSTER_PARAMS` with `n_jobs` 8, one fit at a time on the whole machine, which is what every reported figure in this repository was fitted under.

Nothing here is a reported figure. `scripts/fast_lane.py` writes only under `data/processed/fastlane/` and this one file, and checks at the end of every run that it wrote nowhere else.

## What these runs establish

- **December at the reference condition (8 threads).** 25 of 25 rungs reproduce the ladder's stored residuals exactly.
- **December at 1 thread** (2 rungs). 2 grew different trees: `weather-nolag` by 158.3 s, `throughput` by 40.27 s; 0 differ in the last bits only and 0 are bit-identical.
- **December at 1 thread, 8 at once** (25 rungs). 3 grew different trees: `weather-nolag` by 158.3 s, `throughput` by 40.27 s, `corridor-nolag` by 4.972 s; 13 differ in the last bits only and 9 are bit-identical.
- **December at 4 threads** (25 rungs). 1 grew different trees: `corridor-nolag` by 4.972 s; 7 differ in the last bits only and 17 are bit-identical.
- **December at 8 threads, 2 CPUs** (2 rungs). None grew different trees: 2 bit-identical, 0 differing in the last bits only.
- **December at 8 threads, 4 CPUs** (2 rungs). None grew different trees: 2 bit-identical, 0 differing in the last bits only.
- **December: what a reader would see.** No rung's RMSE moves at the one decimal the reports print, over every condition run (25 rungs).

- **July at 1 thread** (25 rungs). None grew different trees: 21 bit-identical, 4 differing in the last bits only.
- **July: what a reader would see.** No rung's RMSE moves at the one decimal the reports print, over every condition run (25 rungs).

The feature-group count is empty because this LightGBM does not print it on this path at the verbosity these runs used. The column is kept rather than dropped so that its absence is on the record: it was asked for, and it is not there to be had from the log.

**The mechanism is not what separates these rungs.** Of the 6 rungs fitted with the log kept, 6 build a multi-value bin group and 3 grow different trees at another thread count, and the two sets are not the same. Building one without moving: `corridor`, `throughput-nolag`, `weather`. The sparse rate does not separate them either: 0.865997, 0.874267 are each reported by a rung that moves and by one that does not. All 6 of them built such a group and then used none of it, which is what the *not used* in the table means and what `force_col_wise` asks for. So which rungs move at another thread count is **unexplained**: the measurement in the tables above stands on its own, and no mechanism is claimed for it.

Every one of the 4 fits made on a narrowed set of CPUs gives the ladder's own digest. The row blocks the histograms are summed over are therefore cut by `n_jobs` and not by the number of CPUs the process can see, so the reproduction claim is a claim about a fixed thread count rather than about an eight-core machine.

## One condition, one fit

6 rung-and-condition pairs were fitted more than once, and each gave one digest every time. 6 of those pairs hold both a fit made with `--lgbm-verbose` and a quiet one, which is what says that raising LightGBM's verbosity to log the dataset construction left the model alone: a `verbose` that changed the fit would have shown up as a disagreement here.

## What this does not establish

- Any LightGBM other than the one these runs were made with. The library's own documentation says `deterministic` "should ensure the stable results when using the same data and the same parameters (and different `num_threads`)"; the tables above are what this version did.
- Any rung not fitted here, and any split other than the ones named.
- Any machine other than the reference one. Where a run was narrowed with `taskset` it was narrowed on that same machine, which is a different thing from another host.
- A GPU. Nothing in this repository has been fitted on one.
