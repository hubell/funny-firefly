# The geometry claim, month by month

Generated by `scripts/report_monthly_pairs.py` from the held-out-pair folds `scripts/fit_monthly_pairs.py` refitted and stored under `data/processed/monthly_pairs/`; nothing is refitted, every figure is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier. **It decides nothing.**

## What this is

`reports/baseline.md`'s *Held-out pairs*: 3 folds of `taxiout.validation.held_out_pair_splits`, each training on the whole year minus a third of every airport's (airport, stand, runway) pairs and scoring those pairs in every month, beside a 5% sample of rows whose pairs were seen and which were themselves out of the fit. So every month's rows are already in the design; what no file held was their per-row predictions, which `run_baselines.run` keeps in memory for these folds. `scripts/fit_monthly_pairs.py` refitted the three folds by `run_baselines.run` itself for three rungs: `identifiers + time` and `identifiers + time + geometry` (the claim, `run_baselines.CLAIM`) and `+ plan differences` (the shipped rung, read for its gap alone), with `keep_residuals` off and `run_baselines.LADDER` pointed at the three rungs for the call and restored.

**The fits are the ladder's**, checked before anything was read, from the stored files alone: 26 of 26 comparisons held at the printed precision (against `reports/baseline.md`: the fold-mean seen and held-out figures, the gaps and the gaps per fold of the three rungs; the claim on seen pairs, on held-out pairs and as a difference with its interval; and the per-airport claim table row for row).

**The statistic is the claim's own definition, not a margin.** Within each fold, each rung's RMSE on the held-out pairs and on the seen sample; the figure is the mean over the three folds (the *fold-mean* `reports/baseline.md` defines), and the claim is the difference in differences, how much more the geometry block improves the held-out side than the seen side. Restricted to a month, the same statistic is computed over that month's rows on both sides. These are means of per-fold RMSEs by the claim's own definition, so `reports/monthly_ladder.md`'s rule that margins are pooled and never averaged does not apply to them. The interval is the claim's: the 95% percentile interval of a cluster bootstrap over pairs, 1,000 draws under `taxiout.validation.SEED`, resampling the pairs present in the restricted rows. Rows the record-quality term covers are out, as in `reports/baseline.md`. Beside each figure, the second reading over the rows whose recorded taxi-out is 4 h or less, a diagnostic. A cell whose seen side holds under 30 rows in any fold prints its counts only.

This is not a nested design: no fold was refitted per month, and a month's held-out pairs are the fold's, not pairs chosen within the month. A month here is a slice of the three folds' rows.
