# Five seeds per held-out month

Generated by `scripts/report_monthly_seeds.py` from the residuals of `scripts/fit_monthly_seeds.py` under `data/processed/monthly_seeds/`, the held-out-month fits' files under `data/processed/monthly/` and the held-out-month ladder's under `data/processed/monthly_ladder/`; nothing is refitted, every figure is computed there, and the generator refuses to write a movement identifier or a literal stand identifier. **It decides nothing.**

## What this is

`+ plan differences` (`SUBMISSION_KEY`) on each of the twelve held-out-month splits of `reports/monthly.md` and `reports/monthly_ladder.md` (one month of 2025 held out, the other eleven training, so not forward in time), under 5 seed offsets on `BOOSTER_PARAMS["random_state"]`, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, as `run_baselines`' seed-averaging section fits them. Offset 0 is the held-out-month fit's file (`reports/monthly.md`) and was not refitted; offsets 1 to 4 were fitted by `scripts/report_monthly.py`'s own `fit_leg` with only the offset moved. Everything else, the record-quality term on the training months, `fit_rows` with the 4 h exclusion, the shrunk encoding and `BOOSTER_PARAMS`, is the ladder's.

**The five-seed average is the model that ships.** `taxiout.models.SEED_AVERAGE_ADOPTED` is `True` and the submission is the mean of `BAGGING_SEEDS` = 5 boosters whose seeds run consecutively from the ladder's. Its residual here is the mean of the five residuals, the recorded taxi-out minus the mean of the five predictions. `reports/monthly.md` and `reports/monthly_ladder.md` scored one seed.

The floor is the standard deviation of the five single-seed figures times the square root of two (`run_baselines.NOISE_FLOOR_FACTOR`), as `reports/baseline.md`'s *The noise floor* defines it: the spread of a difference between two single-seed fits whose seed noise is independent. Every margin is pooled from the rows it names; nothing is a mean of RMSEs. Beside every scored figure, in brackets, the same reading over the rows whose recorded taxi-out is 4 h or less, a diagnostic, with the count it leaves out.

## Each block's months, against each month's floor

`reports/monthly_ladder.md` counted a block as helping in a month when its change, excluding flagged rows, printed below zero. Here each change is set against that month's floor from the table above, on the unrounded figures: a change more negative than minus the floor helps by more than the floor, one more positive than the floor hurts by more than it, and anything between sits inside it, which is within what changing the seed alone produces. **The floor is measured on the shipped rung and applied to every block's change, which is the same approximation `reports/baseline.md` makes** (its *The noise floor*: the spread is measured on one rung and assumed for the others; the reference rungs draw no seed, so where one is on a side the floor overstates the seed's share). With 5 seeds the standard deviation is itself uncertain by about 35%, as that section computes it. The changes are recomputed from the held-out-month ladder's residuals, and each is checked against the figure `reports/monthly_ladder.md` printed.
