## The truncated first hours

`taxiout.windows.coverage_start` reads the blocks of covered months from the frame itself. The training frame is one block, January to December 2025; the ranking frame is two, January to February 2026 and June to July 2026. A flag is set where its feature's window reaches outside the row's block:

- `win_truncated`: the trailing 60 min before take-off reaches before the block's start.
- `sched_truncated`: 60 min either side of the scheduled time reaches outside the block.
- `lag_truncated`: 15 min either side of take-off reaches outside the block.
- `occ_truncated`: no earlier arrival at the stand was found, and take-off is within 24 h of the block's start.

Counted on the ranking frame as `scripts/make_submission.py` builds it, and on the training frame's first and last months, the two edges of its one block. A month is the calendar month of the take-off time in UTC, as `coverage_start` reads it; the share is of that month's departures. No feature is changed by this, and nothing is decided on it.
